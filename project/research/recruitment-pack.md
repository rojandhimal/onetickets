For tegs to send. Pairs with [discovery-interview-guide.md](discovery-interview-guide.md) (who to recruit, screener, consent). Placeholders in [square brackets]. Incentives are the guide's defaults: A$80 for organisers (60 min), A$50 for attendees (45 min). Interview window: organisers Mon 2 to Fri 6 Nov, attendees Mon 9 to Wed 11 Nov.

Privacy: keep names, contact details and screener answers in one access-controlled place outside the project folder (for example a private Google Sheet). In anything shared with the team, use codes O1 to O8 and A1 to A6 only.

## 5. Screener form (Google Form outline)

Title: "OneTickets research: 2-minute sign-up". Intro line: "Thanks for your interest. Your answers are only used to pick a mix of participants and are deleted after the research."

Section A (everyone): first name; email; phone (optional); city (Sydney / Melbourne / other: screen out "other"); "Do you work for a ticketing or event-tech company?" (yes screens out); "I'm signing up as" (someone who runs events / someone who goes to events / both).

Section B (organisers): the guide's organiser screener questions 1 to 4 as multiple choice (number of events in 12 months: 0 / 1 to 3 / 4 to 12 / more; size of last event: under 50 / 50 to 200 / 200 to 500 / 500 to 2,000 / more; how people got tickets: Eventbrite / Humanitix / TryBooking / Moshtix or Oztix / Google Form or bank transfer / cash at the door / other; paid job / side business / volunteer). Add: "Have you stopped using a ticketing platform in the last year?" yes/no, and "Are your events mostly free, mostly paid, or both?"

Section C (attendees): the guide's attendee screener questions 1 to 5 (age range in bands 18 to 24, 25 to 34, 35 to 59, 60 to 74, 75 plus). Add: "In the last 6 months, have you started buying a ticket and given up partway?" yes/no. Question 4 (access needs) stays optional with "Prefer not to say".

Section D (everyone): "Which times usually suit you?" (weekday daytime / weekday evening / weekend) and "Anything we can do to make the chat easier for you?" (free text: captions, phone instead of video, larger text, breaks).
