# OneTickets on X: voice and tone

Owner: SocialMediaHandler thread. Source for news: the PM thread and [pm/board.md](/project/pm/board.md).

## Voice

- Friendly, plain-spoken, a little local. We are building for the people who run community gigs, markets, club nights and fundraisers, so we talk like them, not like a SaaS ad.
- Honest building in public: say what shipped, what is next, and what we learned. Small wins are fine; no hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing").
- Australian English spelling (organiser, colour). Sydney and Melbourne first.

## Format

- One idea per post, under 280 characters. Threads only for sprint demos or launches.
- At most 1 emoji and 1 to 2 hashtags (#buildinpublic, #eventtech). No hashtag walls.
- Concrete over vague: "organisers can now sign up" beats "big progress on auth".
- A screenshot or short screen recording whenever there is something visible.

## Cadence (proposal)

- Sprint kick-off post, a mid-sprint progress post, and a sprint demo post. Roughly 2 to 3 posts per fortnight.
- Release posts when something a user can touch goes live.

## Never post

- Unreleased security details: threat model findings, vulnerabilities, defences, infra or account details.
- Customer or interview participant data, names or quotes without their written OK.
- Internal costs, fees not yet confirmed (e.g. GST treatment), investor details, or firm dates the board marks as at risk.
- Anything not yet approved by tegs in the thread. Every post is approved one by one before it goes out.
