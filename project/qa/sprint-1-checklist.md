# Sprint 1 QA checklist: publish and browse

Owner: QA thread. Sources: the acceptance criteria in the [sprint backlog](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/908caae3-7868-4e89-9a10-4da3f5d376ae), the design handoff [handoff-s1.md](/project/design/handoff-s1.md) (canvas v17 to v23), the board's SEC-3, and the test strategy in [test-strategy.md](test-strategy.md). Prepared on 10 Oct 2026, before any S1 PR exists. Statuses: ⬜ not run, ✅ passed, ❌ failed, ⏸ needs staging.

Every story also has to meet the Definition of Done:

- CI runs against real Postgres.
- Screens work at 375 px, by keyboard and with a screen reader. axe at 375 and 1280 px is enforced by e2e, with no horizontal scrolling and no CSP violation.
- The module boundary rule passes.
- A money or inventory change comes with an invariant test.
- Unfinished work sits behind a flag.
- The repo docs are updated in the same PR.

"Auto" means a test in CI; the layer is in brackets.

## Notes for the team

- The backlog says the checklist "blocks publishing", and the design keeps the button enabled with an error notice. QA reads these as compatible: publishing must be refused by the api, and the button stays clickable to explain why (1.8).
- The banner is shown by default pending tegs (`showBanner`). If tegs says no, 3.4 and 3.7 switch to the tint fallback.
- 3.2, 3.3 and 3.7 (real chat-app previews) need staging. They stay ⏸ until AWS exists, and Sprint 1 acceptance will list them as deferred, as Sprint 0 does.
